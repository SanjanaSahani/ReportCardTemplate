Mark Distribution in Scholastic Area
1. Annual exam of each paper will be of 80 marks. It will be scaled to 50

2. 20 marks will be awarded on the basis of performance in Half-yearly.

3. 10 marks each will be awarded on the basis of performance in P.T-1 and P.T-2.

4. Maximum mark for class assessment is 10.
  • 5 Marks is to be taken from performance in MCT in whole year.
  • 5 Marks is for Class Notes of all phases.
Marks Distribution in Olympiad Aptitude
1. Maximum marks (Exams) in LGT conducted during exams shall be 120 (for classes 1 to 4) and 180 (for class 5). Here, we shall follow relative marking system. Average of top 5% students of the class shall be scaled to 150 Marks. Consequently Students marks shall be scale as above process.

2. 60 marks will be awarded on the basis of performance in Half-yearly.

3. 30 marks each will be awarded on the basis of performance in P.T-1 and P.T-2.

4. 8 LGTs conducted during 3rd and 4th phase will be of 80 (for classes 1 to 4) and 120 (for class 5) marks each. In combined, it will be of 320 (for classes 1 to 4) and 480 (for class 5) marks. Same process as above will be repeated for 30 Marks of Class assessment.
